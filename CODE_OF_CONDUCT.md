# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We aim to make participation in FORKLAB welcoming and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome beginner questions and explain technical terms where practical.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, and persistent disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and may restrict participation when necessary. Report concerns privately to a maintainer.
